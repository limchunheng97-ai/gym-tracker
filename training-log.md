# Training Log

## Profile
- Male, 29, 180cm, 77kg
- Retired professional Muay Thai fighter, training recreationally alongside a corporate job
- Intermediate lifting experience (6mo-2yrs)
- Goal: body recomposition (build muscle, lose fat) + maintain/improve Muay Thai conditioning and skill
- Tracks with a Fitbit (no direct app integration — shared manually)

## Coaching workflow
1. Each week, tell me which days you're doing Muay Thai — I build the gym/running schedule around them and post it under "Current Week" below.
2. After each gym session, log weight/reps in the [gym tracker app](index.html) (or just tell me in chat) — I set the next target and update this file.
3. Default target shape: 4 gym sessions/week (Upper 1, Upper 2, Lower 1, Lower 2), 3+ Muay Thai, 1 easy run, at least 1 full rest day. If a week's Muay Thai days don't leave room for all of that without a rest day, trim a gym session (usually a Lower day) rather than the rest day — flag it if you'd rather keep all 4 gym sessions and skip the rest day instead.

Design principles: keep lower-body gym days away from any combo (double-session) day so leg fatigue doesn't bleed into kicking; any combo day is immediately followed by a full rest day; running stands alone rather than compounding with a lifting day.
